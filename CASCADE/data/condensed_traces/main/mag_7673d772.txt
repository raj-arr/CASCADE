[T13] WebSurfer:
Rule 601 of the Federal Rules of Evidence establishes that every person is generally considered competent to be a witness unless specific rules provide otherwise. In civil cases, state law determines the competency of a witness if state law supplies the rule of decision for a claim or defense. The rule effectively eliminates older grounds for incompetency—such as religious belief, criminal conviction, or connection to the litigation. The 2011 amendment to Rule 601 was part of a restyling effort to make the language more coherent and uniform across the rules; thus, no substantive changes or deletions were made to the rule itself in the latest amendment.
